AI Automation in Action

Automation that solves a real problem

A current client project, shown as a workflow rather than a list of tools.

Customer feedback & reputation system

Multi-location car care and auto repair business · n8n · 2026 – present

TRIGGERService job completed
ACTIONFeedback request sent automatically
AISentiment scoring
ROUTENegative feedback goes to the right team
ESCALATERepeat-customer issues flagged
LLMReply drafted for staff review
IN PROGRESSLocation-level dashboard

The problem

Staff followed up on feedback by hand, so negative reviews could be slow to reach the right person across several locations.

The solution

An n8n system that requests feedback when a job closes, scores sentiment with AI, routes by rules, and drafts replies that staff approve.

Tools

n8n, LLM integration and rule-based routing logic. Further integrations will be listed once confirmed.

Outcome

Less manual follow-up and a human always in the loop. Measured results will be added once the client approves sharing them.

Client details are kept confidential. Sanitized diagrams and a walkthrough demo can be added here.
